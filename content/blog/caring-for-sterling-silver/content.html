Why Sterling Silver Tarnishes

Sterling silver (92.5% pure silver + 7.5% copper) naturally tarnishes over time. This isn't a defect — it's a chemical reaction with sulfur in the air.

Daily Habits

  • Put on jewelry last. Perfume, lotion, and hairspray accelerate tarnish.
  • Remove before swimming. Chlorine and salt water damage silver.
  • Store separately. Silver scratches easily. Use soft pouches.

Cleaning Methods

Gentle Polish (Weekly): Use a soft, lint-free cloth. Buff in straight lines.

Deep Clean (Monthly): Mild dish soap in warm water. Soak 5 min, brush gently, rinse, pat dry.

What NOT to Do

  • Never use baking soda or toothpaste — too abrasive.
  • Never use ultrasonic cleaners on silver with gemstones.
  • Never submerge gold-plated silver in cleaning solutions.
