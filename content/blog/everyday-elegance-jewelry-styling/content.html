Wear It, Don't Save It

At YUAN YI, we believe jewelry is meant to be worn. The best pieces become part of your personal signature.

The Rule of Three

Three points of interest forming a triangle: stud earrings + pendant necklace + a mid-weight ring.

Daisy-Chain Stacking

  • Rings: Start with 2-3 thin bands on one finger.
  • Necklaces: Combine 2-3 chains of graduated lengths.
  • Bracelets: Mix metal finishes for texture.

Office to Evening

  • Start with a base layer: studs + pendant + commitment ring.
  • Keep a power piece in your bag for evenings.
  • Add one layer to your stack for after-hours.

Mixing Metals

The old rule is outdated. Modern styling embraces mixed metals — choose a base metal and accent with another for depth.
