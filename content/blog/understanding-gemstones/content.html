Moonstone: The Stone of New Beginnings

Named for its ethereal, moon-like glow, moonstone has been treasured since ancient times. Our moonstones are ethically sourced from Sri Lanka.

Freshwater Pearls: Nature's Elegance

Created by living organisms, each pearl forms when an irritant enters an oyster's shell. Our pearls are cultivated in China's Zhejiang province.

Garnet: The January Birthstone

Deep red garnet has been prized for over 5,000 years. Symbolizes energy, passion, and commitment.

Aquamarine: Ocean in a Stone

The March birthstone ranges from pale blue to deep teal. Said to calm the mind and soothe relationships.

Care by Stone Type

  • Moonstone: Delicate. Avoid ultrasonic cleaners.
  • Pearl: Very soft. Never submerge. Wipe with damp cloth.
  • Garnet/Aquamarine: Durable. Safe for most cleaning methods.
