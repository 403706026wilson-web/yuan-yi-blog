Stage 1: The Spark of Inspiration

Every piece of YUAN YI jewelry begins with a hand-drawn sketch. Our designers draw inspiration from architecture, nature, art, and the quiet beauty of everyday moments.

A single collection might begin with over 50 sketches before narrowing down to 8-10 pieces.

Stage 2: Precision Modeling

The winning sketch is translated into a high-resolution 3D model. Art meets engineering — every angle, curve, and proportion is perfected digitally.

Stage 3: Master Craftsmanship

The digital model becomes physical through investment casting. A wax model is created, encased in plaster, and heated to melt away the wax — leaving a perfect mold for molten silver or gold.

But the machine does only 60% of the work. The remaining 40% — hand-filing, surface finishing, stone setting — is done by master artisans.

Stage 4: Quality & Beyond

Every piece undergoes three rounds of inspection. Structural integrity, surface finish, stone security, and overall aesthetic harmony are checked before a piece reaches your hands.
