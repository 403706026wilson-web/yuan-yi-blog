The 4 Cs: Your Starting Point

When shopping for an engagement ring, the "4 Cs" — Cut, Color, Clarity, and Carat — are the industry standard for evaluating diamonds and gemstones. But here's the secret no one tells you: cut matters most. A well-cut stone reflects light beautifully, making even a modest carat weight look extraordinary.

At YUAN YI, every engagement piece is hand-selected for optimal cut quality. Our gemologists inspect each stone under magnification to ensure fire and brilliance that will last a lifetime.

💡 Pro Tip: You can often get a larger-looking stone by choosing a slightly lower color grade (G-H) and a very good cut.

Metal Options: Choose Your Foundation

The metal band is the foundation of your ring. Each option brings its own character:

  • 925 Sterling Silver — Hypoallergenic, brilliant white shine, easy to maintain.
  • 18K Gold Plated — The warmth of gold without the premium price tag.
  • Platinum — The rarest and most durable, never needs replating.

Setting Styles

  • Solitaire — Classic, timeless, lets the stone shine.
  • Halo — Extra sparkle and visual impact.
  • Three-Stone — Symbolizes past, present, and future.
  • Vintage/Art Deco — Ornate details, milgrain edges, unique character.

Sizing Matters

Ring sizes vary by finger, hand dominance, and even time of day. We recommend measuring at the end of the day when fingers are at their largest.
