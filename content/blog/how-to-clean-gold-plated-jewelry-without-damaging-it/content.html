Understanding Gold Plating

Gold-plated jewelry features a thin layer of gold bonded to a base metal (usually sterling silver or brass). This gives you the look of solid gold at a fraction of the price. But the gold layer is delicate — typically 0.5 to 2.5 microns thick — and requires gentle care.

Cleaning Do's

  • Wipe after every wear — Use a soft, dry cloth to remove oils and sweat before storing.
  • Gentle soap bath — Mix a drop of mild dish soap in lukewarm (not hot) water. Dip a soft cloth, wring it out, and gently wipe the piece. Pat dry immediately.
  • Microfiber buff — Use a clean microfiber cloth to restore shine. Avoid rubbing too hard.

Cleaning Don'ts

  • Never use jewelry cleaning dips — Chemical dips can strip the gold layer.
  • Never use abrasive cloths — Baking soda, toothpaste, or paper towels scratch the plating.
  • Never soak overnight — Water can seep between the plating and base metal, causing separation.
  • Never use ultrasonic cleaners — The vibrations can loosen the plating.

Storage Tips

Store each gold-plated piece separately in a soft pouch or compartmentalized box. Moisture is the enemy — add a silica gel packet to your jewelry box. Avoid storing in bathrooms where humidity fluctuates.
