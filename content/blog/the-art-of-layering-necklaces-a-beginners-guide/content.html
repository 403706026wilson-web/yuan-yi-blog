Why Layer? The Power of Multiple Chains

Layered necklaces have become a signature style in modern jewelry — and for good reason. Multiple chains create visual interest, frame the face beautifully, and allow you to express your personality through mix-and-match combinations.

At YUAN YI, we design our necklaces with layering in mind. Each piece shares proportions and aesthetic DNA that make them naturally compatible.

Chain Lengths: The Foundation

The key to successful layering is graduated lengths. Here's the standard formula:

  • 14-16" (Choker) — Sits at the base of the neck. Perfect as your topmost layer.
  • 18" (Princess) — Falls at the collarbone. The most versatile length, works with any neckline.
  • 20-22" (Matinee) — Sits below the collarbone. Ideal for pendants and statement pieces.
  • 24-36" (Opera) — Can be worn long or doubled up. Adds drama to evening looks.
💡 Pro Tip: Start with 3 chains of graduated lengths (e.g., 16" + 18" + 22") for the classic layered look. Add or remove based on your neckline.

Pendant Placement

When layering with pendants, position them so they don't overlap or tangle. A good rule: your shortest chain (choker) should be plain or have a tiny charm, the middle chain carries your statement pendant, and the longest chain has a complementary accent.

Mixing Textures and Metals

Don't be afraid to mix — a delicate gold chain + chunky silver link + beaded wrap creates a rich, curated look. YUAN YI's collections are designed for mixing. Our silver and gold finishes share design DNA even when the textures differ.

Remember: layering is personal. There are no rules — only what looks and feels right to you.
